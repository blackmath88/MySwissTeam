# Demo script — 3 to 5 minutes

## 1. Hook

"Most political apps start by telling you who is speaking. We do the opposite."

Open landing page.

> Gschwätz — Meet the argument before the label.

## 2. Topic

Show one referendum / public issue.

"We start with a short neutral explanation. You do not need to declare your position."

## 3. Meet personas

Show 3 unlabeled avatars.

"They disagree. But you do not know who represents what."

Choose one.

## 4. Conversation

Ask:

> "What matters most to you here?"

Then:

> "What is the strongest argument against your own position?"

Highlight that the avatar acknowledges a genuine trade-off.

## 5. Reflection

Show:

> "Did anything become harder to answer?"

Explain:

"We do not score whether you changed your mind. The point is whether you understood the disagreement better."

## 6. Reveal

Click reveal.

Show sources and political origin.

"Only now do we show where the arguments came from. The persona is fictional. The claims are grounded in public sources."

## 7. Close

"Gschwätz is not an AI that tells you what to think. It is a conversation architecture that helps you meet an argument before the label."
