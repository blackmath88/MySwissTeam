# Branding

## Master brand

# Gschwätz

Swiss-German, informal, memorable, social.

The word can mean chatter or talk, including somewhat pointless talk. The brand can reclaim it:

> Maybe we need better Gschwätz.

## International descriptor

**Let's Talk**

Possible lockups:

- Gschwätz — Let's Talk
- Gschwätz — Meet the argument before the label
- Gschwätz — Politics, before the politics

## Recommended tagline

> **Meet the argument before the label.**

## Voice

- curious
- direct
- non-institutional
- not activist
- not technocratic
- warm without forced friendliness
- never smug

## Core copy

### Landing

**Gschwätz**

Meet the argument before the label.

No quiz. No party matching. Just a conversation with perspectives that do not fit neatly into boxes.

**Let's talk**

### Topic intro

> You don't need to decide yet.

### Avatar screen

> They disagree. You don't know who represents what.

### Reflection

> Did anything become harder to answer?

### Reveal

> Now meet the sources behind the conversation.

### Source disclaimer

> These are fictional AI personas grounded in public sources. They are not official representatives of any party or organisation.

## Visual direction

Avoid:

- red-vs-blue split screen
- ballot-box clichés
- political podiums
- party colours as primary UI
- debate-show aggression

Prefer:

- conversational faces / simple avatar silhouettes
- warm neutral background
- subtle differentiated persona accents
- cards that feel like people, not party cards
- source reveal as a transparent evidence layer

Visual metaphor:

**conversation first, labels underneath**
